Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about working with me

What is your approach to data engineering projects?
I follow a structured approach:
1. Discovery: Understanding business requirements and data landscape
2. Design: Architecture design with scalability and cost in mind
3. Development: Agile development with iterative releases
4. Testing: Comprehensive testing including data quality checks
5. Deployment: CI/CD implementation with monitoring and alerts
6. Optimization: Continuous performance tuning and cost optimization
How long does a typical data engineering project take?
Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity:

• Simple ETL Pipeline: 2-4 weeks
• Data Warehouse Setup: 1-3 months
• Real-time Streaming Platform: 2-4 months
• Complete Data Platform: 3-6 months

I provide detailed estimates after understanding your specific requirements during the discovery phase.
Which cloud platforms do you specialize in?
I have extensive experience with both AWS and GCP:

AWS: S3, EMR, Glue, Redshift, Athena, Lambda, Kinesis
GCP: BigQuery, Dataflow, Cloud Storage, Pub/Sub, Vertex AI, Dataproc

I can help you choose the right platform based on your requirements, budget, and existing infrastructure.
Do you provide ongoing support after project completion?
Yes! I offer various support options:

• Documentation: Comprehensive technical and operational documentation
• Knowledge Transfer: Training sessions for your team
• Maintenance: Ongoing support and maintenance packages
• Monitoring: Setup of alerts and monitoring dashboards
• Optimization: Periodic performance reviews and improvements
How do you ensure data security and compliance?
Security and compliance are top priorities:

• Encryption: Data encryption at rest and in transit
• Access Control: IAM policies and role-based access control
• Compliance: Experience with GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2 requirements
• Auditing: Comprehensive audit logging and monitoring
• Data Governance: Implementation of data quality and lineage frameworks
• Security Reviews: Regular security assessments and updates
What is your pricing model?
I offer flexible pricing models to suit different needs:

• Project-based: Fixed price for well-defined projects
• Hourly: Hourly rate for consulting and smaller tasks
• Retainer: Monthly retainer for ongoing support

Contact me to discuss your specific requirements, and I'll provide a detailed proposal with transparent pricing.
